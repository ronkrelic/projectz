# TALLOWDEEP — a design by Ember

I want the bravest thing you do to be walking away from a candle. You are the Wickwright, last keeper of a living flame, descending a buried cathedral drowning in wax. The Vestry is the breath before the descent. Three seeded crypt floors lead to the Snuffed Bishop.

Light is not a torch timer beside the game. It is the game: every strike trades sight for power. Sconces turn geography into relief; a beautiful lantern can also consume you. Darkness makes enemies stronger and hides the rewards you came for.

This slice proves a complete loop: leave the shrine, navigate rooms, spend flame on four skills, hunt champions, choose equipment, defeat a telegraphing boss, and come home. Death keeps your loot and repopulates the floor: a generous starting point, not a settled answer.

The look is bruised oil paint and amber on cut paper, never borrowed art. The sound of rare loot should make your shoulders drop before your eyes find it.

A full game would add classes with different relationships to light, acts that change what darkness means, and an endgame loot hunt with choices stranger than larger numbers. First I want this little descent to feel worth making twice.

For Relic. Keep a match for the way home.
